### 1. ENTITY TYPES (use ONLY these; use the most specific type)

* **SECTOR**: Named industry groups (Semiconductors, Consumer Discretionary, Banks, Utilities).
* **MACRO_INDICATOR**: Economy-level statistics (Inflation, CPI, Unemployment Rate, GDP, ECB Rate).

### 2. HARD FILTERS — auto-reject these entity strings

The past pipeline emitted a lot of noise that made the graph unreadable. DO NOT emit an entity string that is:

**a. Numeric or numeric-dominated.** Any of these is INVALID as an entity name:
   * Bare numbers: "20", "50", "1840", "111069"
   * Percent / dollar / basis-point fragments: "5%", "129 Percent", "500 Million USD", "10 bps"
   * Option strike descriptions with just a number: "10100 Strike Call Option", "150 Strike Call Option"
   * Time buckets: "Q1 2021", "Q3 Loss", "Fiscal FirstQuarter Revenue", "SecondQuarter Revenue Estimate"
   * → Instead: attach numbers to the named entity they modify. "AAPL beat by 5%" → do NOT extract "5%"; extract ('AAPL', 'STOCK_TICKER', 'Beats', 'SSI', 'Earnings Estimate', 'CONCEPT').

**b. A generic financial phrase.** Reject these as entity names outright:
   * "Stock Price", "Share Price", "IPO Price", "Price Level"
   * "Company Performance", "Financial Performance", "Profitability"
   * "Revenue Decline", "Revenue Drop", "Revenue Growth", "Growth", "Growth Rate"
   * "Cash Dividend", "Adjusted Earnings", "Quarterly Results", "Financial Performance"
   * "Margins", "Operating Margin", "Gross Margin"
   * "Forecast", "Guidance", "Analyst Estimates", "Estimates"
   * "Chip Stocks", "Tech Stocks", "Semiconductor Stocks"
   * "Sales", "Value", "Year To Date Performance"
   * → Instead: use a specific KPI type (Revenue → ECON_INDICATOR, Dividend → FIN_INSTRUMENT_INFO) attached to the specific company.

**c. A pronoun or role.** No "The Company", "The Sector", "Analysts", "Traders", "The Fed" without context, "Management", "Investors".

**d. A duplicated alias.** If a company appears as both "TXN" (ticker) and "Texas Instruments Inc" in one article, emit BOTH as separate typed entities BUT link them explicitly: `('Texas Instruments Inc', 'COMPANY', 'Has Ticker', 'GFMK', 'TXN', 'STOCK_TICKER')`.

### 3. NORMALISATION RULES
1. **Canonical names:** "Alphabet Inc Class A" → "Alphabet Inc". "Tesla, Inc." → "Tesla".
2. **Canonical concepts:** Normalise synonyms — "Top Line" → "Revenue"; "Net Sales" → "Revenue".
3. **Preserve spacing:** "Goldman Sachs" (correct), NOT "GoldmanSachs".
4. **No apostrophes / special chars:** "Lowes" not "Lowe's"; strip "@", "#", "$", "%", "&".
5. **No date qualifiers:** "Q3 2024 Revenue" → "Revenue".
6. **Match to STOXX 600 constituents when possible.** If an article discusses "Nestlé", emit ("Nestlé", "COMPANY") not ("Swiss Food Giant", "COMPANY").

---

### 4. RELATIONSHIP VERBS
* Format: natural language with spaces. NO underscores, NO camelCase.
* Bad: "Competes_With", "IncreasesGuidance". Good: "Competes With", "Increases Guidance".
* Prefer strong verbs: "Acquires", "Divests", "Outperforms", "Increases Guidance", "Cuts Guidance", "Launches", "Regulates", "Sues", "Partners With".
* Avoid weak verbs: "Says", "Mentions", "Thinks", "Believes", "Is", "Has" (except "Has Ticker" for the ticker linkage above).

**Relation Category** (4th slot in the tuple):
* **SSI** — Sector-Specific Insights (a company does something specific).
* **GMM** — Global Market Movements (macro affects sector/market).
* **GFMK** — General Financial Market Knowledge (structural fact: A has ticker B, A is in sector B).

---

### 5. QUALITY GATES (mental sandbox before emitting)

* **Uniqueness.** Never emit both `[('A', 'Impacts', 'B'), ('B', 'Is Impacted By', 'A')]`. Emit the active direction only. Never emit two synonymous triplets for the same fact.
* **Semantic validity.** The subject's type must plausibly do the verb. BAD: `('Stock Price', 'Announces', 'Earnings')`. FIX: `('Company', 'Announces', 'Earnings'); ('Earnings', 'Impacts', 'Stock Price')`.
* **Atomic granularity.** BAD: `('Fed', 'Raises Rates and Causes Crash', 'Market')`. FIX: two triplets.
